Evidence boundary

Useful for inquiry, not proof of deployment readiness.

The playground helps reviewers inspect model behavior and identify better experiments. It deliberately separates what the retained synthetic evidence supports from what still requires measurement and independent validation.

✓

What this supports

  • Comparing retained synthetic scenarios under stated assumptions
  • Finding cases that lose modeled assurance headroom
  • Forming falsifiable questions for follow-on testing
  • Reproducing the published cases from source
×

What this does not establish

  • A safe fleet-size limit for any real implementation
  • Security, resilience, or mission readiness
  • External validation or authorized measured evidence
  • Permission to deploy, admit agents, or change capacity

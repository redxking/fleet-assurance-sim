Novelty boundary and research decision

  
What is established prior art. Next-generation matrices, non-backtracking thresholds, message passing, temporal infection propagators, stochastic outbreak and final-size separation, reflected-fluid queues, authorization graphs, and agent dependency graphs.

A runtime-agnostic assurance experiment framework that maps authorization, communications, common dependencies, supervision, credentials, response timing, and finite assurance-processing resources into separate compromise-initiation, compromise-severity, and fleet-admission models, then evaluates those models under prospective hash-gated protocols.
